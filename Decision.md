## 1. Why this ingestion strategy over the obvious alternative you rejected?

For the homepage, I treated “ingestion” as how Frame brings together different pieces of work and turns them into one clear product story. Instead of going with the typical SaaS approach—feature cards, made-up metrics, testimonials, and a generic dashboard image—I chose to show real UI fragments and an interactive demo. This felt more authentic to the product and closer to the brief.

## 2. One trade-off under the time limit, and what I would do with a real week

With the limited time, I focused on getting the visual consistency, responsive layout, and core interactions right rather than trying to build a fully simulated version of the app. With a full week, I would spend more time on keyboard accessibility, complete tab functionality, a more stateful demo experience, and browser-based visual regression testing across the required breakpoints.

## 3. Where AI tools were used, and what I personally verified or changed afterward

I used AI to help with the initial React component structure and to get ideas for the copy and some CSS details. After that, I personally reviewed and adjusted the implementation, including the layout, spacing, anchor-link behavior, mobile experience, interactive states, reduced-motion support, metadata, favicon, and the no-fabricated-social-proof approach. I also verified that the TypeScript/Vite production build worked correctly.
